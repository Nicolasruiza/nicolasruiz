Product decisions

File by the receipt's date

A backlog photographed in one night still lands in the right months.

Unsure means review

Under 0.6 confidence goes to _Review. A wrong category costs more than an unfiled one.

Batch, not real time

Nobody waits on the AI, and failed calls retry next hour.

A link key, not logins

Two people, one business account, no Google sign-in on the phone. The key can upload, never read.
